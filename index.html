FAQ

Artificial Grass Questions

How much does artificial grass cost in Miami?

It depends on the turf, the size of the area and the site preparation. Because we sell at wholesale prices, our quotes are among the most competitive in Miami. Call (786) 329-9117 for a free estimate.

How long does artificial turf last?

Quality turf lasts 15+ years with normal use. Our installations are covered by a 5-year warranty.

Does it get too hot in the Florida sun?

All turf warms in direct sun. We offer cool-touch fibers and infills that reduce surface temperature, and a quick rinse cools it down instantly.

Is it safe for dogs and kids?

Yes. Our turf is lead-free and non-toxic. Pet turf uses a fully permeable backing and antimicrobial infill to control odors.

What happens during heavy rain?

Water drains straight through the perforated backing and the compacted base — no puddles, no mud.

How long does installation take?

Most residential yards are completed in 1–3 days.

Can I buy turf without installation?

Yes. We sell rolls at wholesale prices to homeowners, landscapers and contractors.
