FAQ

Artificial Grass Questions

How much does artificial grass cost in Miami?

It depends on the turf, the size of the area and the site preparation. Because we sell at wholesale prices, our quotes are very competitive. Call (786) 329-9117 for a free estimate.

How long does artificial turf last?

Lifespan depends on the product, sun exposure and use — we will show you the manufacturer's specifications for the turf you choose. Our installations are covered by a 5-year limited warranty.

Does it get too hot in the Florida sun?

All turf warms in direct sun. Lighter-colored fibers and some infills help, and a quick rinse with the hose cools the surface down.

Is it safe for dogs and kids?

Artificial turf is widely used in backyards, schools and dog parks. We will give you the manufacturer's product and safety information for your turf. Pet turf uses a permeable backing and an antimicrobial infill to help control odors.

What happens during heavy rain?

Water drains straight through the perforated backing and the compacted base — no puddles, no mud.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the size of the area and the site preparation. Your written quote includes the planned schedule.

Can I buy turf without installation?

Yes. We sell rolls at wholesale prices to homeowners, landscapers and contractors.
