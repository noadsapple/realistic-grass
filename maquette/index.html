FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does artificial grass cost?

It depends on the turf, the size of the area and the site preparation. We sell at wholesale prices — call for a free estimate.

Is it safe for dogs and kids?

We'll give you the manufacturer's product and safety information for your turf. Pet turf uses a permeable backing to help control odors.

What happens during heavy rain?

Water drains through the perforated backing and the compacted base.

Can I buy turf without installation?

Yes — rolls at wholesale prices for homeowners, landscapers and contractors.
